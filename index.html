Advancing Interest and Knowledge in Pharmacy

LabPath is a student-led organization dedicated to practical sciences and experiment-based learning. We bridge pharmaceutical science with everyday life.

Innovation

We believe that progress in pharmacy begins with creativity. By exploring new ideas and testing original hypotheses.

Collaboration

Great science is never done alone. Our club thrives on teamwork, where members support one another.

Scientific Integrity

Guided by honesty, precision, and ethical responsibility. We value the trust that comes from accurate data.

Community Impact

Our vision extends beyond the laboratory. We aim to use our work and knowledge to positively affect our school, local communities, and society by raising awareness of pharmacy’s importance in health and daily life.

Lifelong Learning

Pharmacy is an ever-evolving field. We see every experiment, project, and challenge as an opportunity to deepen our understanding, strengthen our skills, and grow into future leaders who continue learning well beyond the classroom.
